Minerva

Minerva works as a merchant dealing in gold bullion and is protected by her bodyguard, Tommy Ten-Toes and her brahmin Minos. She has traveled with the Blue Ridge Caravan Company in the past, and has worked with Aries.Her caravan sells "recreations" of rare plans and recipes, and she claims to have a photographic memory, which helps with these recreations. She draws the plans of guns and other items herself.
